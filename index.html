Eaglercraft server FAQ

Quick answers to the things players ask most before they jump in.

Is ArchyverseV free to play?

Yes - ArchyverseV is completely free. Open the launcher in your browser and you're in. Optional ranks and cosmetics are available in the shop, but nothing is pay-to-win.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. ArchyverseV runs on Eaglercraft, a browser version of Minecraft. There's nothing to download and nothing to install - just click Play and the game boots in the tab.

Does it work on a school Chromebook?

Yes. Because it loads as a normal website, ArchyverseV works unblocked on most school Chromebooks and locked-down laptops. The 1.8.8 client is the lightest and runs smoothest on low-end devices.

Do I need a Minecraft or Mojang account?

No account is required to play in the browser. Pick a username, choose a skin and join - ArchyverseV is cracked-friendly for Eaglercraft players.

What is the Eaglercraft server IP?

Add wss://eagler.carrot-craft.org in any Eaglercraft client to connect directly. On Java use java.carrot-craft.org, and on Bedrock use bedrock.carrot-craft.org.

Which Minecraft versions are supported?

You can play in the browser on Minecraft 1.8.8 or 1.12.2 (WASM or JavaScript). Java Edition players are supported on 1.8 through the latest releases.
